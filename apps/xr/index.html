Welcome to Spatial Lingo · WebXR

A rebuild of Meta's Unity Spatial Lingo mixed-reality language demo, ported to the open web with the Immersive Web SDK. Same idea — learn words by looking at the things around you — but running as a plain web page instead of a headset-only app.

Learn an object's Spanish name and the language tree grows. Ten words takes it from sapling to full canopy.

  1. 1
    Drag to look around the room, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  2. 2
    Tap or click an object — the couch, the lamp, the plant — to start its lesson.
  3. 3
    Type the Spanish word and press Enter. Three tries, then the answer.
